1. System Overview & Architecture

Core Mission & Capabilities

LedgerCore Health is an enterprise-grade Core Accounting, Asset Lifecycle, and Healthcare Facility Management (CMMS) software system engineered specifically for hospitals, clinics, and healthcare networks operating under the OHADA (Organisation pour l'Harmonisation en Afrique du Droit des Affaires) jurisdiction and international accounting frameworks (IFRS).

Dual-Framework Accounting (OHADA & IFRS)

Unlike conventional ERPs that require redundant data entry or parallel databases, LedgerCore Health utilizes a unified double-entry transactional engine that captures each event once and evaluates it across both standards: - Statutory Presentation (OHADA / SYSCOHADA Revised): Formatted for local legal compliance, tax filings, and regional CEMAC/UEMOA banking compliance. - Investor & International Presentation (IFRS): Formatted for international donors, development banks, and corporate stakeholders. - Dual-Standard Bridge: Real-time reconciliation quantifying every difference (e.g., IFRS 16 lease capitalisation, fair value adjustments, depreciation period variances).

Biomedical & Healthcare CMMS Integration

LedgerCore seamlessly links medical equipment (CT scanners, ventilators, surgical autoclaves) with the General Ledger. When a biomedical engineer conducts maintenance or when an asset is depreciated, the operational and financial records remain permanently synchronised with zero variance between the physical register and the balance sheet.

4. Module 2: Chart of Accounts & Analytical Structure

SYSCOHADA Revised Classification (Classes 1–9)

LedgerCore Health implements the complete SYSCOHADA Revised Chart of Accounts structured into 9 statutory classes: - Classe 1: Comptes de ressources durables (Capitaux propres, emprunts, subventions d'investissement). - Classe 2: Comptes de l'actif immobilisé (Immobilisations corporelles, incorporelles et financières). - Classe 3: Comptes de stocks (Médicaments, consommables médicaux, pièces détachées). - Classe 4: Comptes de tiers (Fournisseurs, clients hospitalisés, personnel, État et organismes sociaux). - Classe 5: Comptes de trésorerie (Banques, caisses régies, mobile money). - Classe 6: Comptes de charges des activités ordinaires (Achats médicaux, salaires, entretiens). - Classe 7: Comptes de produits des activités ordinaires (Prestations de soins, hospitalisations, subventions). - Classe 8: Comptes des autres charges et autres produits (Charges et produits Hors Activités Ordinaires - HAO). - Classe 9: Comptes des engagements hors bilan et comptabilité analytique hospitalière.

Posting Level Constraints & Child Account Generator

To maintain statutory integrity, LedgerCore enforces the Niveau d'Imputation Rule: - Heading Accounts (Comptes de Regroupement): 2-digit and 3-digit accounts (e.g., 60, 401) are non-postable (is_postable = 0). Postings made directly to heading accounts are rejected. - Configurable Posting Length: System administrators can set the posting length (typically 4, 6, or 8 characters). - Default Child Generator: If a user selects a heading account, clicking Create Default Child automatically creates the first compliant postable sub-account (e.g., under 4011, it creates 401100).

IFRS Line Mapping Configuration

Every postable SYSCOHADA account has a corresponding 1-to-1 IFRS presentation mapping: - Example: SYSCOHADA 2411 (Matériel médical) maps to IFRS Property, Plant and Equipment - Medical Equipment. - Example: SYSCOHADA 6611 (Rémunérations directes au personnel) maps to IFRS Employee Benefits Expense.

Analytical Cost Centres (Departments & Wards)

To satisfy internal cost management, charges in Class 6 require an assigned Cost Centre: - Clinical Wards: Surgery, Intensive Care Unit (ICU), Maternity, Outpatient, Cardiology. - Support Services: Medical Laboratory, Pharmacy, Biomedical Engineering, Administration.

5. Module 3: Journal Entries & AUDCIF Statutory Capture

Statutory Principles (AUDCIF Articles 17–24)

LedgerCore Health complies strictly with the OHADA Uniform Act (AUDCIF): 1. Article 17 (Pièce Justificative Obligatoire): Every entry must have a supporting voucher reference (e.g., Invoice number, Receipt number, Bank advice). Unreferenced manual entries are prohibited. 2. Article 17 (Partie Double): Total Debits must exactly equal Total Credits. Every entry requires at least two lines. 3. Article 17 (Interdiction des Montants Négatifs): Negative numbers in debit or credit fields are rejected. Adjustments must be made by swapping debit and credit positions or through contra-entries. 4. Article 19 (Comptabilité Auxiliaire): Collective accounts (401, 411, 421) must specify the individual third-party partner. 5. Article 20 (Intangibilité des Écritures): Once an entry is validated and posted, it cannot be modified or deleted. Errors are corrected strictly via contra-entry (extourne).

Specialized Journals (Journaux Divisionnaires)

LedgerCore provides 10 standard auxiliary journals: - ACH — Journal des Achats (Supplier invoices for pharmaceuticals, medical supplies) - VTE — Journal des Ventes (Patient billing, clinic consultations, insurer billings) - BQ — Journal de Banque (Bank deposits, transfers, direct debits) - CA — Journal de Caisse (Cash desk receipts and petty cash expenses) - OD — Journal des Opérations Diverses (General accruals, adjustments) - IMM — Journal des Immobilisations (Asset acquisitions, WIP capitalisation) - AMO — Journal des Amortissements (Monthly and year-end depreciation runs) - PAI — Journal de Paie (Monthly wage and social charge integrations) - CHG — Journal des Charges (Miscellaneous recurring overhead charges) - AN — Journal des À-Nouveaux (Opening balances carried forward)

Capturing a Double-Entry Voucher

  1. Navigate to Accounting -> Journal Entries and click New Entry.
  2. Select the Journal Code (e.g., ACH for Purchases).
  3. Set the Entry Date (must be within an open period).
  4. Enter the Reference * (e.g., FAC-2026-9912 from the supplier invoice).
  5. Enter the Description of the transaction.
  6. In the Lines Table:
    • Choose the debit account (e.g., 6011 — Fournitures médicales). Enter amount. Select the Cost Centre.
    • Choose the counterpart credit account (e.g., 4011 — Fournisseurs).
    • Select the Third Party / Partner (e.g., [FRN] Air Liquide Santé).
  7. Inspect the Out of Balance Indicator: The badge displays 0.00 in green when balanced.
  8. Click Save as Draft or Save and Post (requires journal.post permission).

Auto-Counterpart Suggestion Engine

When typing the first line of a transaction, the intelligent auto-propose engine evaluates transaction templates and historical postings: - Entering an expense line automatically suggests the default balancing liability or treasury account. - The user can accept the proposal or modify lines as necessary.

Posting, Immutability & Reversals (Extourne)

  • Drafts: Can be reviewed, edited, or deleted prior to validation.
  • Posting: Commits lines to the General Ledger, assigns a sequential entry number, and locks the record.
  • Reversals (Extourne): Click Reverse on any posted entry. Enter the reversal date and mandatory justification. LedgerCore automatically generates an inverted contra-entry, linking both transactions in the audit trail.

6. Module 4: General Ledger, Auxiliary Subledgers & Lettrage

General Ledger Account Statements

The General Ledger view displays running movements and balances for any account across any chosen timeframe: - Filter by date range, journal, or analytical cost centre. - Running balance is recalculated instantly with drill-down to the originating voucher.

Statutory 8-Column Trial Balance

Accessible via Accounting -> General Ledger -> Trial Balance, the statutory OHADA trial balance presents 8 columns: 1. Soldes d'ouverture Débit (Opening Debit Balance) 2. Soldes d'ouverture Crédit (Opening Credit Balance) 3. Mouvements Période Débit (Period Movements Debit) 4. Mouvements Période Crédit (Period Movements Credit) 5. Cumul Exercice Débit (Year-to-Date Cumulative Debit) 6. Cumul Exercice Crédit (Year-to-Date Cumulative Credit) 7. Solde de Clôture Débit (Closing Debit Balance) 8. Solde de Clôture Crédit (Closing Credit Balance)

Auxiliary Third-Party Subledgers (Clients & Fournisseurs)

  • Collective general ledger accounts (e.g., 401100, 411100) can be expanded into individual partner cards.
  • View individual supplier aging, outstanding invoices, and payment histories.

Lettrage (Open-Item Invoice & Payment Matching)

Lettrage allows accountants to match vendor invoices against payments: 1. Select the third-party account in the General Ledger. 2. Filter for Unmatched (Non-lettrées) entries. 3. Select the invoice credit line and the corresponding bank payment debit line. 4. Click Match (Lettrer). LedgerCore assigns an uppercase letter code (AA, AB...) and records the reconciliation timestamp.

7. Module 5: Accounting Periods, Fiscal Year & Year-End Close

Period Statuses (Open, Closed, Locked)

  • Open: Standard transactional state; all authorized clerks can capture and post entries.
  • Closed: Standard month-end closure; general postings blocked. Posting requires the journal.post_closed permission and a mandatory written audit justification.
  • Locked: Finalized state; sealed against all modifications after the year-end audit is completed.

Structured 12-Step Close Checklist

Before closing a monthly or annual period, the system enforces a comprehensive pre-close checklist: 1. All drafts in the period posted or cleared. 2. Bank and mobile money reconciliations completed with zero unreconciled variance. 3. Petty cash registers audited and reconciled. 4. Monthly depreciation runs executed and posted to General Ledger. 5. Monthly payroll entry posted and reconciled against DIPE. 6. Withholding tax declarations generated. 7. Patient billing (DFE) reconciled against pharmacy and ward logs. 8. Foreign currency monetary assets/liabilities revalued at month-end closing rates. 9. Inter-branch accounts (181/185) reconciled with zero discrepancy. 10. Inventory count variances booked. 11. Analytical cost allocations verified. 12. Final Trial Balance verified for equilibrium.

Cryptographic Audit Sealing (SHA-256 Chaining)

When a period is locked, LedgerCore executes a cryptographic blockchain-style hash seal: - Every journal line is hashed using SHA-256, incorporating the previous entry's hash, timestamp, user ID, and entry contents. - Any attempt to tamper with the database at the SQL level breaks the hash chain, immediately alerting auditors to data manipulation.

9. Module 7: Fiscal Compliance & Withholding Tax (WHT / TSR)

Cameroon / CEMAC Tax Retentions

LedgerCore incorporates statutory tax retention schedules: - TSR (Taxe Spéciale sur le Revenu): Withheld on foreign technical assistance, IT licenses, and foreign medical consultancy. - Prélèvement sur Achats / Acompte d'Impôt: Withheld on local supplier payments without a certified tax number (Attestation de Non-Redevance). - Precompte TVA: Automatic retention of VAT for designated withholding agents.

Generating WHT Certificates

  • Under Accounting -> Withholding Tax, click Generate Certificate.
  • Filter by supplier and date range. The system formats and prints the statutory CEMAC deduction certificate to accompany payment.

14. Module 12: Asset Depreciation & Leases (IFRS 16)

Automated Monthly & Annual Depreciation Runs

  1. Navigate to Assets -> Depreciation.
  2. Click Run Depreciation. Choose the fiscal period.
  3. LedgerCore computes statutory straight-line or degressive depreciation line-by-line across all active assets.
  4. Click Simulate to review calculations; click Post Run to generate the automated entry in Journal AMO.

Donated Asset Subsidy Amortisation (Accounts 14 -> 799)

For equipment acquired through grants or donor gifts: - The initial equipment cost is balanced in Account 14 (Subventions d'investissement). - Each depreciation run simultaneously generates the subsidy write-back entry (Debit 14 / Credit 799), ensuring that donor-funded assets have a neutral impact on the hospital's net operating result.

IFRS 16 Lease Accounting

  • Leased medical imaging systems or buildings are recorded as Right-of-Use (ROU) Assets with corresponding lease liabilities.
  • Automatically generates the monthly split between principal liability repayment and interest expense.

16. Module 14: Hospital CMMS, Work Orders & Calibration

Preventive & Corrective Maintenance Workflows

  1. Breakdown Request: Ward nurse or doctor logs an issue (e.g., "Surgical autoclave door seal leaking").
  2. Triaging & Approval: Biomedical manager reviews, sets priority (Urgent / High / Medium), and assigns a technician.
  3. Execution: Technician performs maintenance, logs parts used from inventory, and records labor hours.
  4. Verification & Closeout: Biomedical manager signs off after testing functionality.

Electrical Safety (IEC 60601) & Metrological Calibration

To ensure clinical safety: - Record protective earth resistance, chassis leakage current, and patient lead isolation tests. - Upload third-party calibration certificates for incubators, centrifuges, and radiation equipment. - Automatic Quarantine: If calibration expires or safety testing fails, the asset is automatically marked Restricted / Quarantined on the dashboard and cannot be scheduled for patient procedures.

21. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) & Cheat Sheets

SOP 1: Daily Cash Desk Balancing

  1. At shift close, the cashier accesses Treasury -> Cash Registers.
  2. Counts physical bills and coins; enters breakdown into the Denomination Sheet.
  3. Reconciles against receipt vouchers issued.
  4. Transfers excess cash to the hospital vault or bank deposit box.

SOP 2: Month-End General Ledger Close

  1. CFO verifies all clerks have completed their daily journal postings.
  2. Run automated bank reconciliation for all bank and mobile money accounts.
  3. Execute the monthly asset depreciation run (Assets -> Depreciation).
  4. Review the 12-Step Close Checklist under Accounting -> Periods.
  5. When all checklist items show green checkmarks, click Close Period.
