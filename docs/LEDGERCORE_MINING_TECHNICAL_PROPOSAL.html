🛰️ Concession Edge-Sync: Zero-Interruption Offline Pit Operation

Remote extraction pits frequently suffer from satellite latency or fiber dropouts. LedgerCore employs an Offline-First Edge Architecture. Field supervisors can log work orders, dispatch spare parts, and verify pre-shift safety inspections locally without internet connectivity.

When satellite/GSM link is re-established, the Concession Edge-Sync automatically streams queued transactions using conflict-free deterministic resolution, ensuring headquarters has exact real-time financial visibility without halting pit operations.

🔒 Institutional Security & Four-Eye Segregation

Mining operations demand rigorous governance to prevent misappropriation of high-value parts, fuel, and capital expenditures. LedgerCore implements:

  • Immutable SHA-256 Journal Sealing: Fiscal period closures produce cryptographically sealed blocks that cannot be altered retroactively.

⚡ Units of Production (UOP) Depreciation

Traditional time-based straight-line depreciation misrepresents heavy mining assets, as wear-and-tear correlates directly with operating intensity rather than calendar months.

Mining-Specific UOP Formula:
Depreciation = (Asset Cost - Salvage Value) × (Hours Run in Period / Total Rated Engine Life)

LedgerCore supports Units of Production (Engine Operating Hours or Mined Tonnage), Accelerated Reducing Balance, and Straight-Line methods, updating Net Book Value (NBV) automatically every billing cycle.

Instantly checks the pit's maintenance budget allocation. If sufficient credits exist, a real-time statutory commitment is registered, preventing unauthorized expenditures.

06 Budgeting & Statutory Commitment Accounting

Public authorities and international joint-venture partners in the extractive sector demand absolute budgetary discipline. LedgerCore embeds a complete Multi-Axial Budgeting and Real-Time Commitment Accounting (Comptabilité des Engagements) engine designed after sovereign West African institutional standards.

📊 Multi-Axial Budgetary Architecture

Budgets are structured across four simultaneous analytical axes:

  • Concession / Mining Pit: Concession North, Pit Alpha, Tailings Dam, Processing Plant.
  • SYSCOHADA Account: Class 6 (Opex: Reagents, Diesel, Maintenance, Salaries) and Class 2 (Capex: Mine Development, Heavy Fleet).
  • Department / Cost Center: Drilling & Blasting, Haulage, Metallurgy, Safety & Environment, Administration.
  • Funding Program / JV Partner: Own Equity, Commercial Debt, Joint Venture Shareholder accounts.

🛡️ Real-Time Overrun Blocking (Contrôle des Engagements)

In traditional ERPs, budgets are evaluated post-facto during invoice entry—when the liability has already been incurred.

LedgerCore enforces Pre-Commitment Hard Blocking: When a purchase order, contractor agreement, or major work order is submitted, the engine checks:

Available = Revised Dotation - Actual Spent - Pending Commitments

⛔ If Request > Available Balance: Transaction is strictly blocked.
