🛰️ Multi-Site Edge-Sync: Zero-Interruption Offline Capability

Remote operating bases and regional plants frequently suffer from satellite latency or fiber dropouts. LedgerCore employs an Offline-First Edge Architecture. Field supervisors can log work orders, dispatch spare parts, and verify pre-shift safety inspections locally without internet connectivity.

When satellite/GSM link is re-established, the Multi-Site Edge-Sync automatically streams queued transactions using conflict-free deterministic resolution, ensuring corporate headquarters maintains exact real-time financial visibility without halting plant operations.

🔒 Institutional Security & Four-Eye Segregation

Heavy industrial operations demand rigorous governance to prevent misappropriation of high-value parts, fuel, and capital expenditures. LedgerCore implements:

  • Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Plant mechanics cannot modify purchase orders; accounting clerks cannot alter maintenance schedules.
  • Dual Authorization (Four-Eye Policy): Credit transfers and asset write-offs require secondary supervisor approval.
  • Immutable SHA-256 Journal Sealing: Fiscal period closures produce cryptographically sealed blocks that cannot be altered retroactively.

  • Componentized Asset Accounting: Compliant with IAS 16 & SYSCOHADA. The chassis, engine, and undercarriage of heavy equipment are tracked as distinct components with separate depreciation schedules.
  • Rugged Asset Identification: Support for industrial metal QR tags, RFID tags, and serial barcoding for rapid field auditing in challenging operational environments.
  • Location & Base Assignment: Real-time tracking of asset assignment across regional sites, operational bays, and standby emergency reserves.

⚡ Units of Production (UOP) Depreciation

Traditional time-based straight-line depreciation misrepresents heavy industrial assets, as mechanical wear-and-tear correlates directly with operating intensity rather than calendar months.

Industrial UOP Depreciation Formula:
Depreciation = (Asset Cost - Salvage Value) × (Hours Run in Period / Total Rated Engine Life)

LedgerCore supports Units of Production (Engine Operating Hours or Production Units), Accelerated Reducing Balance, and Straight-Line methods, updating Net Book Value (NBV) automatically every billing cycle.

06 Budgeting & Statutory Commitment Accounting

Institutional stakeholders, boards of directors, and financial lenders in heavy industry demand absolute budgetary discipline. LedgerCore embeds a complete Multi-Axial Budgeting and Real-Time Commitment Accounting (Comptabilité des Engagements) engine designed after sovereign West African institutional standards.

📊 Multi-Axial Budgetary Architecture

Budgets are structured across four simultaneous analytical axes:

  • Operating Site / Plant: Site Base North, Site Alpha, Secondary Processing Facility, Central Logistics Hub.
  • SYSCOHADA Account: Class 6 (Opex: Reagents, Diesel, Maintenance, Salaries) and Class 2 (Capex: Facility Expansion, Heavy Fleet).
  • Department / Cost Center: Earthmoving, Haulage Logistics, Materials Processing, Safety & Environment, Administration.
  • Funding Program / JV Partner: Own Equity, Commercial Facilities, Joint Venture Shareholder accounts.

🛡️ Real-Time Overrun Blocking (Contrôle des Engagements)

In traditional ERPs, budgets are evaluated post-facto during invoice entry—when the liability has already been incurred.

LedgerCore enforces Pre-Commitment Hard Blocking: When a purchase order, contractor agreement, or major work order is submitted, the engine checks:

Available = Revised Dotation - Actual Spent - Pending Commitments

⛔ If Request > Available Balance: Transaction is strictly blocked.
